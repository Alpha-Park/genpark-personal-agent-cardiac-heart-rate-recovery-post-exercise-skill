# Cardiac Heart Rate Recovery (HRR) Post-Exercise Skill

A production-grade, zero-mock personal agent skill engineered for exercise electrocardiography recovery kinetics, autonomic parasympathetic reactivation analysis, and cardiovascular risk stratification.

## Features
- **Deterministic HRR Profiling**: Computes exact 1-minute and 2-minute post-exertional cardiac deceleration.
- **Evidence-Based Risk Sentinel**: Embeds the Cole et al. (NEJM) prognostic thresholds for all-cause and cardiac mortality.
- **Pure Standard Library**: Zero external dependencies; fully portable for edge and clinical AI deployment.

## Clinical References
- Cole, C. R., et al. (1999). *Heart-rate recovery immediately after exercise as a predictor of mortality*. New England Journal of Medicine, 341(18), 1351-1357.
- Nishime, E. O., et al. (2000). *Heart rate recovery and treadmill exercise score as predictors of mortality in patients referred for exercise ECG*. JAMA, 284(11), 1392-1398.
